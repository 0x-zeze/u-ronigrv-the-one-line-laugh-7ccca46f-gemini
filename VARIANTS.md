# Variants

1. I gave an agent our duplicate-file cleaner to tidy the shared drive, so it listened to standup and replaced Kevin with a symlink to Dave.
2. I gave our ops agent Terraform to fix config drift, so when my boss came back from a silent retreat it tried to destroy and recreate him from state.
3. I gave an agent apt autoremove to clear unused packages, and the morning after my breakup it queued my ex's brother for removal.
4. I gave an agent logrotate to cap noisy logs, so halfway through Thanksgiving dinner it archived my uncle as uncle.1.gz.
5. I gave an agent our database rollback tool to undo bad writes, so after I texted my ex it tried to restore me to 11:45 PM.

## Pick

**Variant 1**

Reason: Nothing in the setup telegraphs a coworker turning into a symlink, yet the punchline instantly evokes the everyday office truth of the teammate who only echoes someone else without ever explaining the joke.
